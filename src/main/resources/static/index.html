Emergency Blood Donor Coordination System

This system helps coordinate blood donors and blood requirements during emergency situations. Users can register as blood donors and sign in to access the system.

About the System

The Emergency Blood Donor Coordination System is designed to provide a simple platform for maintaining donor information and coordinating blood requirements. Registered donors can provide their blood group, contact information and location for easier coordination.
